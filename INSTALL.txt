SURGERY EQ 1.0.11 - Windows x64 VST3

Close your DAW and back up the previous bundle outside scanned folders.
Copy the COMPLETE SURGERY EQ v1.0.vst3 folder into:
C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
Rescan and verify v1.0.11. Keep one scanned copy. Bundle/class IDs are retained.
Unsigned development build; test copies of production projects first.

ANALYZE > VISUAL MODE selects Standard, Storm Symbiote, Synesthesia,
Brooding, Pulse, Aura, Focus or Standard+. Standard remains the default.
SONAR ON/OFF gates analyzer colour/weather overlays; the accurate trace remains.

ANALYZE > JUKE MODE independently selects Real Jukebox, Storm Tube,
Symbiote Flow, Pulse Rail, Ambient Idle or Off. Real Jukebox is the fresh-instance
default. JUKE Off hides all tube energy; the manufactured glass/carbon remains.
SONAR OFF does not turn off JUKE; use JUKE MODE > Off for the border.

Freeze holds both visual simulations. Off controls override visibility while
frozen. Reduced Motion defaults ON and preserves light response with less drift
and turbulence. Silence leaves faint casing reflections after decay.

Old sessions retain the two-choice SONAR automation parameter. The expanded
selector uses a new appended parameter; old projects with Storm+SONAR ON recall
Symbiote Flow, otherwise JUKE Off. Existing controls/geometry and DSP are retained.
See VISUAL_MODES_1_0_11.md and BUILD-NOTES.txt for validation and limitations.
